Privacy

What this site stores

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts and icons are served from this site, so your browser makes no requests to font providers.

The chat

The chat loads only after you select “Send a message”. It is provided by Crisp (Crisp IM SAS), which sets cookies and processes the messages and email address you send. Until you select it, none of this happens.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses under its own privacy statement.
